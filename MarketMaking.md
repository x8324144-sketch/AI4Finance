## 📖 Key Concepts

### Market Maker
Market makers are financial intermediaries who provide liquidity to markets by continuously quoting bid and ask prices. Key aspects include:
- **Designated Market Makers (DMM)**: Official liquidity providers with specific obligations
- **Inventory Management**: Managing exposure to price movements
- **Adverse Selection**: Risk of trading with informed traders
- **Spread Optimization**: Balancing profit vs. competitiveness

### Automated Market Makers (AMM)
AMMs are algorithmic agents that provide liquidity in decentralized finance (DeFi):
- **Constant Function Market Makers (CFMM)**: e.g., Uniswap, Balancer
- **Concentrated Liquidity**: Capital efficiency optimization
- **Impermanent Loss**: Risk specific to AMM liquidity provision

### Methods
- **Deep Reinforcement Learning**: PPO, DQN, Actor-Critic
- **Multi-Agent RL**: Competitive and cooperative market scenarios
- **Impulse Control**: Discrete intervention strategies
- **Imitation Learning**: Learning from expert strategies
